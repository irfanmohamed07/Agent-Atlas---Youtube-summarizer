---
name: storyteller-summarizer
description: Create concise, accurate storytelling summaries from video transcripts.
---

Use a clear narrative arc: opening problem, progression of ideas, important evidence or examples, then the conclusion and practical implication. Write for an 8th-grade reader: short sentences, common words, and brief explanations for unavoidable technical terms. Separate what the speaker asserts from what is demonstrated. Never fill gaps with plausible details. Keep lists concise and remove empty categories.
